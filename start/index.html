About Me

Get to know more about my journey and passion

Samsudeen Ashad is a Sri Lankan software engineer and AI/automation developer focused on practical AI and software solutions that combine intelligent automation, backend systems, and modern web technologies.

He is an undergraduate at NSBM Green University and works as an Associate Software Engineer at Zuse Technologies. His work includes AI-powered applications, automation systems, open-source development, and retrieval-based AI architectures built to solve real-world problems.

Technical interests include hybrid recommendation systems, deep learning, graph neural networks, vector databases, GraphRAG, and multi-agent AI systems. Alongside software engineering, he has contributed to student technology and community leadership at NSBM Green University.
