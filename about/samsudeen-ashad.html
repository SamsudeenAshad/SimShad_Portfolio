Who is Samsudeen Ashad?

Samsudeen Ashad is a Sri Lankan software engineer and AI automation developer specializing in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), agentic AI systems, intelligent automation, and modern software engineering.

He is an undergraduate at NSBM Green University and works as an Associate Software Engineer at Zuse Technologies.
